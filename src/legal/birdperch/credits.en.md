# BirdPerch: bird sound credits

The bird sounds in BirdPerch are real recordings bundled with the app; they play offline. They carry no meaning, translation or training result. Credit does not imply that the recordist endorses this app. Attribution is not required for CC0 and public-domain recordings; we still name the recordist and the source. Every recording was prepared for the app the same way: mixed to mono, levels matched (sounding part −27 dBFS RMS, peak at most −1 dBFS), 10 ms fades at both ends, encoded as AAC-LC 64 kbps. Each recording's own changes are listed below.
