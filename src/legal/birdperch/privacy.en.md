# BirdPerch Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 October 2026

Most of your data in BirdPerch stays on your device. You don't need an account, and there are no ads, no third-party tracking and no sale of data. This policy explains what is processed where, and your rights.

## 1. Who we are

BirdPerch is developed by Ahmet Emin Tayfur, Istanbul, Türkiye. \
Contact: ahmet@ahmettayfur.com

## 2. Data that stays on your device

- Your bird's profile: name, species, colors, perch, theme and gender choice.
- Bird journal: care check-ins, weight entries and notes.
- Voice recordings you make for Voice Practice.
- App settings and widget data.

This data is not sent to our server. If you delete the app, it is removed from your device.

**Talk with my bird:** Your recording is processed entirely on your device, deleted when processing ends, and never sent anywhere.

## 3. AI features (only with your permission)

Asking Gaia, finding colors from a photo and cage analysis ask for your explicit permission the first time you use them. You can withdraw it at any time in Profile > AI permission.

What is sent when you use them:

- **Gaia:** your message, the most recent messages of the chat, and your bird's name, species, gender and age. If you are a Plus member and have given permission, a summary of the last 14 days of your journal (care check-ins, weight entries, notes) is also sent.
- **Color finding and cage analysis:** the photo you choose. Before sending, it is resized on your device and all metadata, including location, is removed. Cage analysis also sends your bird's species.

This content is passed through our server, which runs on Cloudflare, to Anthropic's Claude AI model, and the answer is returned to you. We do not store your messages or photos; our server logs contain only technical details (request type, result and duration), never content. Request logging is turned off at the gateway layer.

Anthropic processes this data under its commercial terms and [privacy policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy) and does not use data received through its API to train models.

AI answers are for information only; Gaia does not diagnose and is not a veterinarian.

## 4. Data we keep to apply usage limits

To apply AI limits (for example, the number of photos per day), our server keeps:

- A one-way hash of the anonymous user ID the app creates for you. We do not keep your name, email or device identifier.
- Usage counts linked to that hash (which feature, which day, how many times).

Your IP address is used momentarily by Cloudflare for rate limiting to prevent abuse; it is not stored in our database.

## 5. Subscriptions and purchases

Payments are handled by Apple; we never see your card details. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status. RevenueCat processes the anonymous user ID, purchase and subscription history, and basic device and app information needed for the service (such as OS version, app version and language). We also record which screen of the app a purchase started from. See RevenueCat's [privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/) for details.

## 6. Permissions

- **Camera:** only when you choose to take a photo.
- **Photos:** we use the system photo picker, so we access only the photo you select, never your whole library.
- **Microphone:** for Voice Practice and Talk with my bird.

## 7. Legal basis

Your explicit consent for AI features, performance of a contract for subscriptions, and our legitimate interest in preventing abuse and applying usage limits.

## 8. International transfers

Cloudflare, Anthropic and RevenueCat may process data outside Türkiye (for example, in the United States). The explicit consent you give for AI features also covers transfers related to those features.

## 9. Retention

- Data on your device: until you delete it or remove the app.
- Messages and photos: not stored on our side.
- Usage counts: as long as needed to apply limits; you can ask us to delete them.

## 10. Your rights

Under Turkish data protection law (KVKK, Article 11) and, if you are in the EU, the GDPR, you have the right to access your data, request correction or deletion, object to processing and withdraw consent.

- Withdraw AI permission in Profile > AI permission.
- Remove on-device data by deleting the app.
- To delete your usage counts on our server, send the Support ID shown in Profile to ahmet@ahmettayfur.com.

## 11. Children

BirdPerch is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children of that age.

## 12. Security

All connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Service keys are kept only on the server, never embedded in the app. We process only the minimum data a feature needs.

## 13. Changes

We may update this policy. For significant changes we will let you know in the app; the effective date is always shown at the top of this page.

## 14. Contact

For questions and requests: ahmet@ahmettayfur.com
