# BirdPerch Support

Ran into a problem or have a suggestion? Write to us at **ahmet@ahmettayfur.com**; we usually reply within 2 business days. Including your phone model, iOS version and app version (at the bottom of the Profile screen) helps us help you faster.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I cancel my subscription?** In Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose BirdPerch Plus. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid being charged.

**How do I move Plus to a new phone?** Sign in with the same Apple account and tap Restore Purchases on the Plus screen. Your bird and journal are stored only on your device, so they are not moved to a new phone automatically.

**Can I get a refund?** Apple handles payments, so refund requests are made at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

**What happens to my bird when Plus ends?** Your Plus-only choices return to their free equivalents (for example, a Plus theme returns to Plain). They come back if you subscribe again.

**Why can I ask Gaia only a limited number of questions?** AI features have usage allowances; you can see your current allowance on the related screen. Daily allowances reset every night.

**Is Gaia a veterinarian?** No. Gaia gives general information and does not diagnose. In an emergency, see an avian vet right away.

**What kind of photo works best for photo analysis?** Choose a sharp photo in good light where a single bird (or the cage) is clearly visible. If there is a spot in the cage you're unsure about (bars, perches), also take a close-up of it.

**How do I withdraw AI permission?** In Profile > AI permission. If you withdraw it, AI features stop working until you give permission again.

**How do I delete my data?** Your bird, journal and settings stay only on your device; deleting the app is enough. To delete the anonymous usage counts on our server, email us the Support ID shown in Profile. See the [Privacy Policy](https://ahmettayfur.com/products/birdperch/privacy/) for details.

**Does Talk with my bird translate my bird's language?** No. It's an entertainment feature that sings back the rhythm and melody of your voice in your bird's chirps; the recording is processed only on your device.

**Sounds aren't playing.** Check that your phone isn't in silent mode and that the volume is up. If the problem continues, write to us.

**The widget isn't updating.** Try removing the widget from your Home Screen and adding it again. If the problem continues, write to us.
