# QR: ScanStash — Support

Last updated: 9 October 2026

QR: ScanStash is an iPhone app for scanning QR codes and keeping a local visual library of web links.

## Contact

For help, bug reports or feature suggestions, email **support@ahmettayfur.com**.

Please describe what you tried, what happened, and your iPhone model and iOS version. Include the app version if you know it. Remove private information from any links or images you choose to send.

## Common questions

### How do I add a link?

Choose **Scan QR**, **Scan photo**, or **Add link** on the main screen. When entering a link, you can type a domain such as `example.com`; the app adds `https://` when needed. You can also share a web link to QR: ScanStash from Safari's share sheet.

### Why does the camera not open?

Check the app's camera permission in iOS Settings. Manual link entry and photo scanning remain available if camera access is disabled.

### Why does photo scanning not ask for full gallery access?

The app uses Apple's photo picker to receive only the image you select. QR recognition runs on the device. If several QR codes are found, you choose which result to use. If the image is in iCloud, it may need to download before it can be read.

### How do saved cards differ from recent scans?

Saved cards remain until you delete them. When scan history is enabled, Recent scans keeps the 20 most recent distinct QR contents. Disabling future history does not erase existing entries. Saved cards and scan history can be deleted separately.

### Where are my links and previews stored?

The app keeps them locally. It has no user accounts or developer-operated cloud sync. Opening a website requires the destination site to be available. A stored page preview is an image rather than an offline copy of the site.

### How do I use the Home Screen widget?

Open a saved card's options and choose the widget help action. Add the QR: ScanStash widget from iOS's widget gallery, then edit the widget to choose its card. After that card is deleted, choose another saved card for the widget.

### Can support recover a lost library?

We do not have a remote copy of your library. The app has no built-in backup or restore feature. Apple's device backups follow your device settings; deleting the app can remove its local data.
