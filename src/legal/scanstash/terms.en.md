## 1. What the app provides

QR: ScanStash is an iPhone utility for scanning QR codes, saving web links with your own titles, keeping optional local previews and opening those links again. You may also add links manually or through the iOS share extension and use a configurable Home Screen widget. The app requires iOS 17 or later. The free plan allows up to 10 saved cards. QR and photo scanning, recent scans, search, previews, copying, sharing and the existing widget remain available without a subscription. An optional one-time ScanStash Pro purchase unlocks unlimited saved cards. There are no advertisements.

## 5. Local storage and recovery

Your library is stored locally. QR scan history is separate from saved cards and is limited to the 20 most recent distinct scanned contents when enabled. We cannot recover your local library from our own server because we do not hold a copy. App removal, device loss, storage problems or other failures can cause data loss. Keep an independent copy of links you cannot afford to lose. The app currently offers no library backup or library restore service; Apple device-backup behavior depends on your settings.
